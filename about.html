Hello, internet

I'm Nicole.

I'm a data scientist based in New York. I spend a lot of time at work trying to understand human behavior through data. Outside of work, I occasionally turn my own questions into very unserious websites.

I'm interested in the things people hold on to: old messages, memories, places, art, and stories. Lately I've also been wanting to write more — about language, books, history, travel, and whatever else I can't stop thinking about.

This is where I keep the things I make. The writing will come when it's ready.

Some things on my mind lately

  • Books and the lives we've never livedEspecially when a fictional person from another time feels eerily familiar.
  • History in ordinary placesHow walking through Seville can make you think about somewhere across the Atlantic.
  • Art and museumsRothko, Yoshitomo Nara, and whatever makes me stay in a room longer than expected.
  • LanguageWhat it means to think and write in a second language.
