Things I'm making, noticing, and becoming.

I'm Nicole, a data scientist based in New York.

I spend a lot of my day thinking about systems, patterns, and decisions. Outside of work, I've been making small internet things out of whatever I can't stop thinking about — old messages, emotional experiences, museums, cities, self-control, and the strange little ways technology keeps pieces of our lives.

This site is part portfolio, part notebook, part evidence that I'm still becoming a person who makes things.

I'm usually thinking about

  • Art, museums, and experiences that stay with me Museo Soumaya in Mexico City. Rothko rooms. Anything that makes a room feel like it's holding its breath.
  • Places and timelines Mexico City, Tokyo, Istanbul. The way a city can feel like a version of yourself you haven't met yet.
  • Dashboards, decisions, and behavior The gap between what data says and what people actually do.
  • How to build a life that feels bigger than work
  • Colonialism, cultural memory, and who gets preserved Reading: Orientalism by Edward Said. Who decides what gets remembered.
  • Yoshitomo Nara The angry girls, the soft rebellion. Art that looks simple but holds something heavy.
  • Cats Always cats.
