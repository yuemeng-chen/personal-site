Should I Date This Man? ↗

My friend and I had heard one too many dating horror stories. At Silly Hack NYC, we turned them into a website to help the girls out — part dating advice, part Mean Girls burn book, and very much a safe space to talk shit.

It won $111. Which feels like exactly the right amount of prize money for this idea.

RIP Graveyard ↗

The data scientist in me initially wanted to analyze the messages from my last relationship: texting frequencies, NLP, all of it. Then I realized I didn't need another analysis of something that was already over.

So I made a graveyard instead. A place to burn the messages, again and again, because it's over. For real this time.
